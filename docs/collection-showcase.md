Each collection starts with a full-width detailed illustrated universe. Below it, a compact pencil-style demo sketch introduces the transformation, followed by figurine, miniature world and acrylic charm in a grid. Product views, a substantial branded gift box and related editions complete the presentation. World, sketch, product and packaging panels all open large inspection dialogs. There is no second showcase in the creator studio.

Nine new assets are optimized WebP files: one sketch, one landscape world and one packaging image per character. Their identities use the earlier approved reference artwork and collection sheet. The Tesla attachment guides ink-and-watercolour richness and substantial retail packaging; its brand or objects are not reused.

The sketches are AI-created reinterpretations and visibly labelled Demo sketch. They are not presented as historic uploads or hand-drawn works by an artist. Lunarie's earlier figurine is a saved verified ARTOY output from its earlier coloured input; the new sketch did not produce that output in a live test. All other supporting artwork remains curated. Prototype/source notes start collapsed; only Lunarie has a paper proof. No manufactured or physically tested unit is claimed.
