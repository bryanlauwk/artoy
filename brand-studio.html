OpenGame CLI Command

After downloading brand-brief.json and placing your mascot/logo images in a brand-kit/ folder, run:
opengame --brand-kit ./brand-kit -p "Build the game" --yolo
Folder structure expected: 
brand-kit/
├── brand-brief.json     ← downloaded above
├── mascot.png           ← your uploaded mascot
└── logo.png             ← your uploaded logo (optional)
What happens next:
① OpenGame reads your brand brief
② ip_to_character tool converts your mascot → pixel-art sprite frames (idle, walk, attack)
③ Brand colors auto-set the style_anchor for all asset generation
④ GDD is generated with your IP name, character, and theme
⑤ Agent scaffolds, codes, and debugs until index.html is playable
